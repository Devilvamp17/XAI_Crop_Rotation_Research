# Coffee Crop Facts
Coffee suitability depends strongly on agro-climatic zone, altitude band, temperature stability, and rainfall distribution. In many contexts, seasonal placement and long-term climate compatibility matter more than short-term point forecasts.

Nutrient management should be based on soil and leaf analysis where possible. Potassium and balanced nutrition influence plant health and bean development, while excess nitrogen can shift growth balance unfavorably in some systems.

High humidity can increase disease pressure if canopy management and airflow are poor. Site-specific practices are critical; generic recommendations are often insufficient.

In model-assisted systems, coffee may appear as a high-probability class under certain input patterns. Always verify regional seasonal suitability and production-system fit before action.
