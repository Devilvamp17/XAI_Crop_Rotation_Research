# Zaid Season Notes
Zaid is often a short summer transition window between major seasons, usually requiring dependable irrigation and heat management.

Short-cycle crops may be suitable, but rapid evapotranspiration and high daytime temperature can increase stress risk. Crop selection should emphasize duration, water availability, and local market/logistics feasibility.

Decision guidance:
- Choose crops with manageable cycle length and known heat tolerance.
- Validate irrigation adequacy before committing area.
- Apply conservative planning when weather uncertainty is elevated.
