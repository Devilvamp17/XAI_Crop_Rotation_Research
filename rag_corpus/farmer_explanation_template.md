# Farmer Explanation Template
When sharing AI recommendations with farmers, prioritize clarity and actionability:

1) What is recommended and why (short reason).
2) How confident is the recommendation (raw vs adjusted confidence).
3) What to check before planting (exactly three actions).
4) What risks remain (season mismatch, uncertain inputs, model disagreement).

Avoid technical overload unless the audience requests detail. If confidence is low, explicitly recommend manual review and pilot testing before full-scale decisions.
