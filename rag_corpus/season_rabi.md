# Rabi Season Notes
Rabi season typically follows monsoon, with cooler temperatures and lower rainfall. Irrigation reliability, cold stress episodes, and terminal heat risk can all influence final crop outcomes.

Wheat and several pulse/oilseed crops may be strong candidates in many regions, but local microclimate and water access remain decisive.

Recommendations:
- Confirm sowing date against local thermal trajectory and irrigation plan.
- Use season-aware ranking to avoid out-of-window crop choices.
- If recommendation confidence is low, prioritize conservative options with strong seasonal fit.
