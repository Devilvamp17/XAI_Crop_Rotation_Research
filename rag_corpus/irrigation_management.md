# Irrigation and Moisture Management Notes
Water reliability is a key hidden variable behind many crop recommendations. Even when temperature and nutrient conditions look favorable, poor irrigation scheduling can dominate final outcomes.

Best-practice principles:
- Match irrigation frequency to crop stage, soil type, and evaporative demand.
- Avoid both chronic deficit and prolonged saturation.
- Track field-level indicators (soil moisture, canopy stress, drainage behavior).

For uncertainty-aware decisions:
- If weather variables are estimated, validate with local measurements.
- Reduce scale of adoption when confidence is low.
- Prefer crops with tolerance to expected moisture variability.

Use irrigation management as a decision stabilizer alongside model outputs and seasonal constraints.
