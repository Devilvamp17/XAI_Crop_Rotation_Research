# Kharif Season Notes
Kharif season is commonly associated with monsoon-aligned cropping, but local onset timing, intra-season dry spells, and flood risk vary substantially across districts.

Crop choice should account for rainfall reliability, drainage behavior, and expected humidity regime. High raw model confidence for a crop with poor monsoon fit should be interpreted cautiously.

Operational principles:
- Align sowing with effective rainfall onset rather than nominal calendar dates.
- Reassess crop suitability if onset is delayed or early-season drought occurs.
- Prioritize crops with proven local performance under variable monsoon behavior.

In decision pipelines, seasonal suitability should act as a hard practical filter over purely statistical preference.
