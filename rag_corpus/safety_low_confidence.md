# Safety Guidance for Low Confidence Recommendations
Low adjusted confidence indicates uncertainty after applying practical constraints (seasonality, data quality, disagreement handling). In this state, recommendations should be conservative and reversible.

Conservative strategy:
- Verify all key soil measurements (especially N/P/K and pH).
- Validate weather assumptions with local observations.
- Prefer in-season crops with robust historical local performance.
- Start with pilot acreage before full deployment.

Escalate to agronomist review when:
- calendar re-ranking changes top crop,
- models disagree materially,
- critical features are estimated rather than measured,
- risk tolerance is low or crop investment is high.

Communication principle: uncertainty is not failure; it is a signal to increase verification before action.
