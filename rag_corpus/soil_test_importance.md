# Soil Test Importance
Reliable soil diagnostics are foundational for agronomic decision quality. N, P, and K should not be inferred from generic assumptions; they should come from measured field samples and context-specific interpretation.

Why testing matters:
- Prevents over/under-fertilization and avoidable cost.
- Improves nutrient-use efficiency and environmental outcomes.
- Reduces model input uncertainty and improves recommendation trust.

Recommended practice:
- Sample representative field zones (not single-point only).
- Record sampling depth and timing consistently.
- Repeat tests periodically, especially after high-yield seasons or major weather disruptions.

When pH or weather is estimated by external APIs, treat recommendations as provisional screening outputs. Final fertilizer and crop decisions should incorporate measured field data and local extension guidance.
